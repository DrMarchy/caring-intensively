CARING INTENSIVELY: billing, ECMO and CPD for Dr Denzil Gill
================================================

This folder is the whole app. It is a static website: no server, no database,
no subscription. Every record and label photo is stored only on the phone that
uses it. The website itself holds nothing but the app's code.

PUT IT ONLINE FOR FREE (about 5 minutes, done once)
---------------------------------------------------

Option A: GitHub Pages (free, permanent)
 1. Sign in or create a free account at https://github.com
 2. Click "New repository". Name it "caring-intensively", choose Public, click Create.
 3. Click "uploading an existing file". Open the Caring Intensively folder, select
    everything inside it (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, the icon
    PNGs, README.txt and the "ocr" folder) and drag it all in together. Wait
    for every file to finish uploading (the ocr folder is about 15 MB), then
    click "Commit changes".
 4. Open Settings > Pages. Under "Build and deployment", choose
    "Deploy from a branch", branch "main", folder "/ (root)", then Save.
 5. After a minute or two the app is live at
    https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/caring-intensively/
    Send that link to Denzil.

Option B: Cloudflare Pages (free)
 1. Sign in or create a free account at https://dash.cloudflare.com
 2. Workers & Pages > Create > Pages > Upload assets.
 3. Name the project "caring-intensively" and drag in this folder. Click Deploy.
 4. The app is live at https://caring-intensively.pages.dev (or a similar address).

INSTALL ON DENZIL'S IPHONE
--------------------------
 1. Open the link in Safari.
 2. Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
 3. Open Caring Intensively from the Home Screen icon (not from Safari) and start there.
    A Home Screen app keeps its own storage, so anything entered in a Safari
    tab won't appear in the installed app.
 4. Choose "Start with an empty logbook", or explore the 12 months of sample
    data first and then tap Clear on the yellow banner.

Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the menu, then "Add to Home screen".

KEEPING RECORDS SAFE
--------------------
 - Records live only on the phone. Use Settings > Back up now regularly and
   save the backup file to iCloud Drive or email it to yourself.
 - Deleting the Home Screen icon, or clearing Safari website data, deletes the
   records. Restore them with Settings > Restore a backup.
 - Patient label photos are health information. Keep the phone locked with a
   passcode or Face ID.

READING PATIENT LABELS
----------------------
After you photograph a label, Caring Intensively reads the name, UR/MRN, health fund and
referring doctor and fills them in for you to check. This runs entirely on
the phone using the open-source Tesseract engine in the "ocr" folder; the
photo is never uploaded. The first time it runs it loads the engine (about
7 MB), so do that once on Wi-Fi. After that it also works offline.

EMAILING REPORTS
----------------
Every report opens as a preview. Tap "Email PDF": the phone's share sheet opens
with the PDF attached. Choose Mail, paste the saved address (it's copied
automatically) into To, and tap Send.

UPDATING
--------
To update the app later, replace the files in the same place (GitHub or
Cloudflare). Denzil's records stay on his phone and aren't affected.
